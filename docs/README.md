# ⚽ ePass Overview

Welcome to the **ePass** documentation! 

ePass is a Web3 social project focused on bringing **accessibility and compliance** between football players and clubs. By leveraging Ethereum smart contracts, we provide a transparent, auditable history of impact actions and verifiable agreements.

---

### 🌟 Our Vision

We aim to democratize access to the football ecosystem. Through decentralized technologies, we bridge the gap between emerging talent and established clubs, ensuring fairness and transparency.

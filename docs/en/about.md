# 🌍 About

## Our Mission 🎯

**ePass** was born out of a critical need: the football ecosystem lacks transparency, making it difficult for emerging players to connect with clubs safely and fairly. Our mission is to introduce **accessibility and compliance** through decentralization.

## Why Blockchain? ⛓️

We use Web3 to solve real-world problems. By deploying smart contracts on the blockchain, we ensure:

* **Immutability:** Once an action or contract is recorded, it cannot be tampered with.
* **Transparency:** Anyone can verify the history of a club or a player.
* **Automation:** Smart contracts automatically enforce the rules of agreements without the need for costly intermediaries.

### The Problem We Solve
Traditional player-club relationships often suffer from a lack of auditable history and opaque contract management. We replace this with an open, verified ledger—driving a social impact that protects the most vulnerable actors in the sport.

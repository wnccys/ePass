# 🏃 Players

**ePass** empowers football players by giving them true ownership of their career data and professional rights. 

## The Player Experience

### Auditable Career Path 📜
Every contract signed, every impact action completed, and every right transferred is securely recorded on the Ethereum blockchain. This means your history travels with you and cannot be altered or hidden by third parties.

### Accessibility 🚪
We lower the barriers to entry. By providing a decentralized identity and verifiable track record, emerging talent can connect directly with clubs that value transparency.

### True Ownership & NFTs 💎
Player rights are tokenized as NFTs. This ensures:
* **Fairness:** You always know where your rights are allocated.
* **Security:** Your professional agreements are protected by the same cryptography that secures the Ethereum network.
